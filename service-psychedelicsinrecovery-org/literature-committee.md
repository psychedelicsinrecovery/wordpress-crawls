## Literature Committee (LitCom)

#### A Bridge Between Experience and Expression

The mission of LitCom is to carry the message of recovery in written form by stewarding the clarity, integrity, and unity of Psychedelics in Recovery™ literature, so the heart of the 12 Steps and 12 Traditions may be expressed faithfully, inclusively, and responsibly for all who seek recovery. Guided by group conscience and loving service.

### The Written Voice of PIR

LitCom serves as a steward of PIR®'s written voice, ensuring published materials reflect PIR®'s Steps, Traditions, Guiding Principles, and shared commitment to safety, inclusivity, unity, and spiritual integrity. Working with the Public Relations and Tech Committees, LitCom reviews, edits, and finalizes written content — newsletter articles, blog posts, scripts, educational materials, member-facing resources — keeping language clear, accessible, compassionate, and aligned with PIR®'s primary purpose.

LitCom's role is not to govern, promote outside issues, or endorse individual opinions, but to preserve coherence, humility, and consistency across PIR® literature.

### Get Involved

No prior literature experience required — just a desire to serve. Typical contributions: writing and editing copy, editing and managing content.

**Email:** litcomm@psychedelicsinrecovery.org

### Guiding Insight

> "Suppose, for instance, that during the last twenty-five years A.A. had never published any standard literature … no books, no pamphlets. We need little imagination to see that by now our message would be hopelessly garbled. Our relations with medicine and religion would have become a shambles. To alcoholics generally we would today be a joke and the public would have thought us a riddle. Without its literature, A.A. would certainly have bogged down in a welter of controversy and disunity."
> — Bill W., The A.A. Grapevine, May 1964; The Language of the Heart, p. 348

### Responsibilities

- Review, editing, and refinement of written material for clarity, consistency, tone, accuracy, and alignment with PIR®'s Steps, Traditions, Guiding Principles, and Safety Guidelines
- Alignment with PIR® core literature (Preamble, Steps, Traditions, Integration language, FAQs, other Conference-approved texts)
- Compassionate and inclusive language stewardship
- Finalization and readiness for publication, coordinating with the PR Committee
- Consistency across platforms (newsletters, blogs, scripts, website, member materials)
- Inter-committee collaboration (PR Committee, Tech Committee, etc.)
- Honoring PIR® unity and primary purpose — watching for language that drifts into endorsement, outside issues, or misrepresentation of PIR® as a fellowship
